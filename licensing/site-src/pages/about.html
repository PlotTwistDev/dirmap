About Filescape

Filescape is a desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux that brings together jobs usually spread across several tools: seeing where your space went, managing files, looking inside them, and understanding the AI models on your computer.

What it does

  • Manage files in two panes with tabs, previews, undo, batch rename and folder compare.
  • See your storage as a zoomable treemap or sunburst, with duplicates, snapshots and explanations of system files.
  • Preview hundreds of formats, from camera RAW and PSD to spreadsheets, fonts, audio, 3D models and Gaussian splats. Supported formats
  • Work with local AI models: find them, see what's inside, check whether they'll run, and chat with them offline. AI models
  • Reach servers and cloud folders: SFTP, FTP(S) and WebDAV, and honest sizes for Google Drive, OneDrive, iCloud Drive and Dropbox.
  • Handle creative projects: frame sequences as one row with missing frames marked, and an EXR and DPX player. VFX and 3D

It doesn't replace every specialist app. A DAW, a 3D package or a dedicated sync tool will always go deeper in its own field. Filescape is for finding, understanding and organising the files those apps make, without opening each one.

What we believe

  • Explore freely. Browsing, mapping, previewing and understanding files is free for everyone, for good, with no account. So is finding and inspecting AI models and talking to them.
  • Stay private. Your files, previews and AI conversations stay on your computer. What the app sends, and when
  • Work smarter with Pro. Filters, batch work, servers, sequences and automation are Pro: one payment, a perpetual licence, no subscription. Pricing

How it's built

Filescape is a native app written in Rust. It isn't a web page in a window. The map is drawn by your graphics card, so it stays smooth with millions of files, and the whole app, including its Help Centre, works without an internet connection.
