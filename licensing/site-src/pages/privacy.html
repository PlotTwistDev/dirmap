Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains what Filescape ("we") collects. You can reach us about privacy through the contact form. This policy covers the Filescape app, filescape.app and the licence server.

The app

Filescape doesn't send anything about your files anywhere. Scanning, previews and Ask AI run on your computer. It only connects to the internet for licensing, update checks, AI models you choose to download, and servers you choose to connect to:

  • Activating a licence sends the serial number, an activation ID, an anonymous identifier of the computer (a one-way hash of its system ID) and the app version.
  • A weekly check, when online, sends the activation ID and the same computer identifier.
  • The update check at each launch asks for the latest version number and sends nothing about you, your computer or your licence. You can turn it off in Settings > General.
  • Downloading an AI model happens only after you agree to it, and fetches the model file straight from where it's published (such as Hugging Face). That site sees an ordinary download request from your computer.
  • Servers you connect to (SFTP, FTP, WebDAV) receive what you send them. Saved passwords are kept in your system's keychain or credential store, not in Filescape's settings.

We use these to enforce the licence's limit, to spot shared serial numbers, and to let you see and release your computers.

Buying

Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record, processes your payment and holds your billing details under its own privacy policy. Paddle tells us your name, email address and the transaction ID so that we can issue and email your licence.

What we keep

  • Licences: owner name and email, the serial number (encrypted, so we can send it again), dates, and Paddle's transaction ID.
  • Activations: the computer identifier, its operating system (Windows, macOS or Linux), app version, and when it was activated, last checked and released.
  • Requests to email serial numbers, for 30 days, to prevent abuse.
  • Messages you send through the contact form: your name (if given), email address, topic, message and our replies, plus a one-way keyed hash of your IP address used only to limit spam. We keep them until the conversation is finished and delete them on request.

We keep licence records for as long as the licence exists, and as long as tax law requires for purchases.

Who processes it

  • Cloudflare: hosts the website and licence server, and stores the licence database.
  • Resend: sends licence emails and our replies to contact form messages.
  • Paddle: payments, receipts and refunds.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion (deleting a licence means it can no longer be activated or recovered). Ask through the contact form.

Cookies

filescape.app sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are served from filescape.app itself. Paddle's checkout script loads only when you start to buy, and Paddle may then set cookies needed for payment and fraud checks. When you sign in at account.filescape.app, it sets one cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days; signing out removes it. Sign-in links are sent to the email address registered to your licence and work once, for 15 minutes.
