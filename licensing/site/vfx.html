Sequences

Frame sequences are one row, not 240

Numbered frames fold into a single sequence with its range, size and any missing frames. Copy, move, rename or delete the whole shot at once, and find every plate with one filter pattern.

  • Padding, steps and gaps detected; frames rendered on twos aren't reported missing
  • Patterns like *_beauty.[1001-1100].exr filter by pass, version or frame range
  • Snapshots and Color by: Change show which shots grew since the last delivery

Player

Play a shot straight from the list

Double-click a sequence to play it from memory with a timeline, loop and ping-pong, at frame rates from 23.976 to 60. Select two and press Enter to compare them with a wipe.

  • EXR, DPX (8 to 16 bit), PNG, TIFF and the other image types
  • Linear to sRGB or Rec.709, Cineon log, exposure in stops, and .cube LUTs from Resolve or Nuke
  • Multilayer EXR render passes, channel by channel, in the preview pane
3D, CAD and splats

Turn a model or a scan without opening an app

STL, OBJ, PLY, glTF, 3MF, FBX, COLLADA and Alembic show as shaded clay with their triangle count and size. Gaussian splat scenes (SPZ and PLY) are drawn for real on your graphics chip, with their splat count, the graphics memory they need, and checks for floaters and stray splats.

  • DXF and DWG drawings drawn with their layers, units and AutoCAD version
  • Blender, Cinema 4D, 3ds Max and SketchUp from the picture they save; Maya and Houdini scene details
  • Photoshop layers, Affinity, Krita and GIMP files, and the Craft Apps
Project media

Which files does this project use?

After Effects projects, music projects from Ableton Live, Logic Pro, REAPER, FL Studio and Cubase, and FilmCraft, SoundCraft and EffectCraft projects list the footage and sound they use, and where each file is.

  • This drive, another drive, a network drive or the cloud, and which are missing
  • Files only in the cloud marked, since opening the project would download them
  • Click a file to show it in Files. Plugins are never loaded
