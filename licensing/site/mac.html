Opening Filescape on a Mac

Filescape isn't notarised by Apple yet, so the first time you open it, macOS stops it and says it can't check it for malware. You only need to let it through once. After that it opens like any other app.

macOS 15 Sequoia and later

  1. Open Filescape-macos.dmg and drag Filescape onto Applications.
  2. Open Filescape from your Applications folder. macOS says "Filescape" Not Opened. Click Done (not Move to Trash).
  3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security. You'll see "Filescape" was blocked to protect your Mac. Click Open Anyway.
  4. Enter your password or use Touch ID, then click Open Anyway once more.

The Open Anyway button only shows for about an hour after you tried to open the app. If it's gone, open Filescape again and go straight back to step 3.

macOS 14 Sonoma and earlier

  1. Drag Filescape onto Applications as above.
  2. In your Applications folder, Control-click (or right-click) Filescape and choose Open.
  3. Click Open in the message that appears.

Advanced: use Terminal instead

If you're comfortable with Terminal, this removes the download flag macOS puts on the app, so it opens straight away. It's also the fix if macOS says Filescape is damaged and can't be opened.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Filescape.app

Letting Filescape see all your files

Once it's open, macOS asks before Filescape reads your Desktop, Documents, Downloads or an external drive, with a message like "Filescape" would like to access files in your Documents folder. Click Allow. Other apps' data, such as Mail, Messages and parts of your Library folder, stays closed even then, so a scan can come up short.

Give Filescape Full Disk Access

For a complete scan, and no more questions folder by folder:

  1. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Full Disk Access.
  2. If Filescape is in the list, turn it on. If not, click +, choose Filescape in Applications and click Open.
  3. Enter your password or use Touch ID if asked.
  4. Quit Filescape and open it again, then scan again.

Filescape shows an Allow Full Disk Access… button in its status bar when a scan couldn't read everything, which opens this page of System Settings for you. Clicked Don't Allow earlier? Turn the folder back on under Privacy & Security > Files & Folders > Filescape.
