The licence

A Filescape Professional licence lets you use the Professional features of Filescape. A perpetual licence doesn't expire. You can move a perpetual licence between your computers by deactivating it on one (in Filescape or at account.filescape.app) before activating another. To stop a licence being passed round, a computer released within 7 days of another release keeps its place until those 7 days pass. For temporary licences, each computer activated uses one of its activations and deactivating doesn't give it back. If you're stuck, contact us.
