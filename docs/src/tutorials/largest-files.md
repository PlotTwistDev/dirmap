# Find the ten largest files

**Goal:** list the biggest individual files on a drive, wherever they are.

## Steps

1. Scan the drive: click it in the drive list (or **🖴 Drives…** if a scan is already open).
2. Look at the treemap. The **largest rectangles are the largest files**; hover over each to see
   its path and size in the status bar, and click it to select it.
3. To narrow it down to only big files, type `1 GB` into **Larger than** in the filter bar and
   choose **Show matches only**. The map now holds only files of 1 GB or more, and the count
   next to the mode buttons says how many there are. Lower the limit (`500 MB`, `100 MB`) until
   about ten remain.
4. Alternatively, open the **Sizes** tab and click the **over 10 GB** or **1 – 10 GB** bar to show
   just that range.
5. Click each rectangle to select it. The folder list opens to show where it lives, with its exact
   size and date.

## Tips

- In the folder list, the largest item of each folder is always first. Opening the first folder at
  each level usually leads straight to a large file.
- Combine with a category: **Show: 🎬 Video** plus **Larger than 1 GB** lists big videos only.
- To see the largest files of one type, right-click that type in the **File types** tab and choose
  **🔍 Find all .ext files**: the list is sorted largest first.
