# Navigate a dense treemap

**Goal:** explore a map with millions of small files without losing track of where you are.

## Steps

1. **Start broad.** Hover over the large regions to read their folder paths in the status bar; the
   faint outline shows the folder of the tile under the pointer.
2. **Zoom towards what interests you.** Point at it and turn the wheel. The point under the pointer
   stays put, so you won't drift away. Labels appear as tiles get big enough.
3. **Pan with a drag** to look around at the same zoom.
4. **Open a folder** by double-clicking one of its tiles when you want to work inside it. The
   breadcrumbs above the map now show the path, and the map lays out just that folder.
5. **Select as you go.** Click a tile, and the folder list opens to show exactly where it is. The
   selection outline stays visible at any zoom.
6. **Step back** with **Alt+←** or the mouse's back button. Each place you stopped at is
   remembered, including how far you were zoomed in. **Alt+→** goes forward again.
7. **Go up** with a right-click: first out to the folder you're in, then up to its parent.
8. **Start over** with **⟲ Whole map** or **Home**.

## Tips

- Lost? Look at the breadcrumbs and the selection's path in the status bar.
- Lost the selection? Press **Zoom to selection**.
- Too busy? Turn off **File names on tiles**, or filter: **Show matches only** removes everything
  you don't need.
- Zooming too fast or slow? Adjust **Zoom sensitivity** in **⚙ Settings > Treemap**.
