# Size histogram

The **Sizes** tab on the right shows how the files in the folder on the map are spread across
size ranges, from **under 1 KB** to **over 10 GB**.

Each range has two bars:

- a **blue bar** for the space those files use, and
- a **grey bar** for how many files there are,

with the exact numbers on the right. Both are scaled to the largest range.

It answers questions such as "is this folder big because of a few huge files or millions of
small ones?". A drive whose space is mostly in the **1 – 10 GB** and **over 10 GB** ranges can
be cleaned up by deleting a handful of files; one whose space is spread across the small ranges
can't.

## Filtering by size

**Click a bar** to show only files in that size range on the map; it sets **Larger than** and
**Smaller than** in the filter bar for you. Click the same bar again to turn it off.

## What it covers

- The folder shown on the map (follow the breadcrumbs), not the whole scan, unless you're at
  the top.
- Only the kinds of files chosen under **Show:**, if any. The Custom filter and size limits
  don't affect it.
- In **Show matches only** mode, only the matching files.
